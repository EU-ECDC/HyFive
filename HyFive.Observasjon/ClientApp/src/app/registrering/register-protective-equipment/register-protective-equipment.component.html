{{sessionView.setting.name}}

You can now observe the professions you selected on the Start observation page, change professions on the individual card by clicking the drop-down menu, or add new professions by clicking the New card button at the bottom right. You register protective equipment by clicking on the icons in the card. If no protective equipment is used, you must save the card without any checkmarks to register the observation. If you find out along the way that an Indicated equipment is Not indicated or vice versa, you can drag and drop the equipment as you wish between the Indicated and Not indicated fields on the card.

When you are finished, you can drag the card to the right to save the observation. If you have registered any errors, you must press "Clear card" - then the card will reset itself. If you want to remove a card from the list, you can drag the card to the left.

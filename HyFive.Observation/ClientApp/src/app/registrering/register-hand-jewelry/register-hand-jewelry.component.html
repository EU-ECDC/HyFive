Jewelry, watches and nails

You can now observe the professions you selected on the Start observation page, change professions on the individual card by clicking the drop-down menu, or add new professions by clicking the New card button at the bottom right. You register hand jewelry by clicking on the icons in the card.

The following requirements meet a valid observation:

Either

  1. Everything OK (No jewelry, watches or long/applied nails)

Or

  1. At least one observed deviation

When you are finished, you can drag the card to the right to save the observation. If you have registered an error, you must press "Clear card" - then the card will reset itself. If you want to remove a card from the list, you can drag the card to the left.

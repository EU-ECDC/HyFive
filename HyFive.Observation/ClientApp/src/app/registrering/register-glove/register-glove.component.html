Gloves

You can now observe the professions you selected on the Start observation page, change professions on the individual card by clicking the drop-down menu, or add new professions by clicking the New card button in the bottom right.

If you are going to observe glove use without indication, you must first click on Gloves without indication.

When you are finished, you can drag the card to the right to save the observation. If you have registered an error, you must press "Clear card" - then the card will reset itself. If you want to remove a card from the list, you can drag the card to the left.
